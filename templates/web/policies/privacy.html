Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how www.vedabrass.com collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit, browse, or make a purchase from our website.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, register on our website, contact us, or interact with our services, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, billing address, shipping address, order details, and payment-related information.

We may also automatically collect certain device information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, time zone, referral URLs, pages viewed, and website interaction data.

How We Collect Information

We collect information through forms, checkout pages, cookies, log files, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies. Cookies help us improve your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and understand how users interact with our website.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process and fulfill orders, arrange shipping, provide invoices and order confirmations, communicate with you, offer customer support, improve our website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

We may also use your information to send promotional updates, offers, or product information, only where permitted by applicable law or based on your communication preferences.

Payment Security

We do not store your debit card, credit card, or sensitive payment details on our servers. Payments are processed securely through trusted third-party payment gateways.

Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties for their independent marketing purposes. Your information is used only for order processing, service communication, website improvement, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Third-Party Services

We may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers such as payment gateways, shipping partners, analytics providers, and marketing tools that help us operate our website and deliver our services.

These third-party services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising and Tracking Preferences

You may manage certain advertising preferences through the following links:

  • Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out

Do Not Track

Please note that we do not alter our website's data collection and usage practices when we receive a “Do Not Track” signal from your browser.

Data Retention

When you place an order through our website, we retain your order information for our records, customer service, legal compliance, and dispute resolution unless and until you request deletion of this information, where applicable.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for marketing communications where applicable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

Jurisdiction

In case of any dispute, the jurisdiction shall exclusively lie with the courts of Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, concerns, or complaints, please contact our support team at support@vedabrass.com.

Vedabrass Policy

Exchange & Return Policy

At www.vedabrass.com, we take pride in delivering handcrafted brass products with exceptional quality and care. Since each piece is artisan-made, slight variations may naturally occur.

We encourage customers to carefully review this Exchange & Return Policy before placing an order.

Return Eligibility

Returns are accepted only in cases where the product received is damaged, broken, defective, or incorrect.

To request a return, customers must contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery along with:

  • Order ID
  • Clear product photos
  • Unboxing video showing the issue

Requests submitted after 48 hours of delivery may not be eligible for return or exchange.

Conditions for Returns

Returned products must:

  • Be unused and in original condition
  • Include original packaging and accessories
  • Not show signs of wear, damage, or alteration caused after delivery

Products purchased during Clearance Sales, Flash Sales, Seasonal Sales, or promotional offers are not eligible for return or exchange.

COD Orders

Returns are not applicable on Cash on Delivery (COD) orders. In case a COD order arrives damaged or incorrect, we may offer an exchange or store credit after verification.

Exchange Policy

Exchanges may be approved under the following conditions:

  • Wrong product delivered
  • Damaged or broken product received
  • Product issue verified by our support team

Customers may exchange the product for:

  • The same product (subject to availability)
  • A different product of equal value
  • A higher-value product by paying the price difference

Exchange requests due to personal preference or dissatisfaction are limited to one-time exchange only.

Store Credit Policy

In cases where replacement stock is unavailable, eligible customers may receive store credit equivalent to the approved order value after applicable deductions such as reverse shipping charges, if any.

Store credit:

  • Can be used for future purchases on Vedabrass
  • Has no fixed expiry unless otherwise specified
  • Is non-refundable and non-transferable

No Refund Policy

We generally do not offer monetary refunds. Eligible cases are resolved through product replacement or store credit only.

Refunds may only be considered in exceptional circumstances where a replacement or exchange cannot be arranged.

Reverse Pickup & Shipping

Once a return or exchange request is approved, our team will arrange a reverse pickup where service is available.

If reverse pickup is unavailable in your location, customers may be requested to ship the product back to our warehouse address.

Shipping or reverse logistics charges may be deducted in cases involving:

  • Incorrect or incomplete shipping address provided by the customer
  • Failed delivery attempts
  • Order refusal by the recipient
  • Non-defective exchange requests

Processing Timelines

Once reverse pickup is arranged, returned items generally take 10-15 business days to reach our warehouse.

After inspection and verification, exchange processing or store credit issuance may take an additional 2-3 weeks.

Customers will receive updates through email or SMS during the process.

Items Not Eligible for Return or Exchange

  • Products purchased during sales or promotional campaigns
  • Customized or made-to-order products
  • Used or damaged products after delivery
  • Products returned without original packaging
  • Requests raised after 48 hours of delivery

How to Request a Return or Exchange

To initiate a return or exchange request:

  1. Email our support team within 48 hours of delivery
  2. Share your Order ID
  3. Attach product photos and unboxing video
  4. Describe the issue clearly

Our support team will review your request and respond within 2-3 business days with further instructions.

Important Notes

Vedabrass reserves the right to approve or reject any return or exchange request after verification.

The final decision regarding returns, exchanges, replacements, or store credit shall solely rest with Vedabrass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund instead of store credit?

Refunds are generally not provided. Eligible cases are resolved through replacement or store credit.

Can I return a product purchased during a sale?

No. Products purchased during sales, clearance events, or promotional offers are considered final sale items.

What if I report the issue after 48 hours?

Unfortunately, requests raised after 48 hours of delivery may not be eligible for return or exchange.

Can COD orders be returned?

COD orders are not eligible for return. Only exchange or store credit may be offered after verification in eligible cases.

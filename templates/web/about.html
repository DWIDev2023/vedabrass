The Wellness Heritage

Pital (Brass): The Golden Legacy of Wellness

For generations, Indian homes have welcomed the warmth and radiance of Pital (Brass), a timeless alloy of copper and zinc that beautifully blends tradition, functionality, and well-being.

More than a metal, brass has long been regarded as a companion to healthy living. From the first sip of water in the morning to family meals shared across generations, brass utensils have been woven into the daily rituals of wellness.

Across centuries, families have cherished brass not only for its beauty and durability but also for its deep connection to traditional Indian lifestyles rooted in mindfulness, balance, and nourishment.

01

For Children

Traditionally, brass vessels have been valued for storing water and serving food, helping families connect with age-old practices that emphasize nourishment and vitality.

02

For Young Adults

The copper and zinc present in brass are essential trace minerals required by the body. Zinc contributes to normal skin health, while copper supports iron metabolism and the formation of red blood cells.

03

For Adults

In Ayurvedic tradition, brass is believed to support the balance of bodily energies and promote overall well-being. Its enduring use in kitchens reflects a heritage of mindful and wholesome living.

04

For Seniors

Brass utensils have remained a trusted part of traditional households, symbolizing strength, longevity, and a connection to practices that support a healthy lifestyle.

Bringing Wellness Home

Every brass vessel, plate, tumbler, lamp, and handcrafted artifact carries a legacy of care passed down through generations. In a world that constantly changes, Pital remains a golden reminder that true luxury lies in living closer to nature, tradition, and timeless wisdom.

Pital is not merely a metal, it is a heritage of wellness, a symbol of vitality, and a celebration of healthy living for every generation.
